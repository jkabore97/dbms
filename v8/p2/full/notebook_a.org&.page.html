    [Sherwin Carlquist field notebook from 1967 expedition to Hawaii]
    Title[Sherwin Carlquist field notebook from 1967 expedition to Hawaii]
    CreatorCarlquist, Sherwin John, 1930-2021
    DescriptionA field notebook containing handwritten notes by botanist Sherwin Carlquist during a field expedition to Hawaii, specifically the islands of Maui, Hawaii, and Oahu, in 1967. The notebook has a spiral binding made of wire along the left edge. The front and back cover are yellow. The notebook has worn edges, dirt stains, and water stains. The contents of the notebook record Carlquist's field observations and voucher specimen collecting events. They include the unique collector numbers used to identify the specimens; collecting dates and locations; navigational directions to specific areas; geographic features and elevations; and stratigraphic details. Carlquist's botanical descriptions detail the plant's natural habitat, taxonomic identification, seasonal observations, weather conditions, plant morphology, abundance and distribution, and associated plant species, insects and other pollinators. His notes also document occasions when he photographed plants or collected liquid-preserve samples. The field notes also include Carlquist’s hand-drawings of plant parts and maps. The notebook covers Carlquist collector numbers 2379-2427.
